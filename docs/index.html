Ge’ez Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · App: Ge’ez Sudoku (com.biruk.amharic_sudoku)

Ge’ez Sudoku is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzles in progress, statistics, collected words and settings are saved only on your device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

No accounts, analytics or ads

The app has no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no advertising. It does not request internet access.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules one local reminder per day. This happens entirely on your device. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Sound and pronunciation

Sound effects are bundled with the app. When you ask to hear a word, the app passes that word to your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Sharing and word reports

When you share a result or report a word, the app opens your device’s share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to send it with, and that app’s own privacy policy then applies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
