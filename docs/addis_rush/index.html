Addis Rush Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · App: Addis Rush (አዲስ ሩጫ), package com.addisrush.addis_rush

Addis Rush is a game. You do not create an account and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (birr, gems, unlocked vehicles, routes and upgrades, best distances, missions, daily challenge attempts) and your settings are saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them. We cannot see or restore them.

Services used by the game

Some features use Google services. They may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in their own policies:

Sound and voice

Spoken lines (for example the minibus conductor calling the route) use your phone's built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded and the game never uses the microphone.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your score and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it.

Purchases

The game does not sell anything with real money in this version. If in-app purchases are added later they will be processed by Google Play and this policy will be updated.

Children

The game is made for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. Analytics data is kept according to Google's retention settings (14 months at most). Because we have no accounts, you can delete everything the game stores by uninstalling it; to ask about analytics data, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above.

Terms of use

Addis Rush is provided "as is" for personal entertainment. Birr and gems are game currency with no real-world value and cannot be exchanged or refunded. Please do not try to cheat, modify or reverse-engineer the game or its leaderboards. Play responsibly and never play while driving or walking in traffic. We may update the game, its content and these terms at any time.

Help: how to play

  • Swipe left or right anywhere to change lane. Swipe up to hop over potholes, puddles, open manholes and speed bumps.
  • Minibuses and buses flash their brake lights (and show a red "!") before stopping, and blink their indicator before changing lane. Roadworks show a sign 2 seconds ahead.
  • Pass close to traffic for a near miss (+25). Five in a row starts Eskista fever: double points and birr for 6 seconds.
  • Power-ups: magnet (pink), shield (blue), turbo (orange), double birr (green).
  • Finish missions to raise your score multiplier. Upgrade power-ups and buy vehicles in the Garage. New routes unlock by distance on the previous route or with birr.
  • Animals are never hurt: if you meet them, they scatter and your run stops.
