## Full description (max 4000)

```
Ge’ez Sudoku is the classic logic puzzle played with Ethiopic fidel (ፊደል) instead of numbers. Fill every row, column and box with each letter once, and one solved row spells a hidden Amharic word. Guess it early for a bonus.

You don’t need to read Amharic to play. Match the shapes and follow the logic, and you will pick up letters and words along the way.

A LITTLE LOGIC. A LITTLE LANGUAGE.
• Daily ፊደል: a new puzzle every day with a themed hidden word
• 4×4, 6×6 and 9×9 boards, from Easy to Expert
• 2,400+ puzzles, each with exactly one solution
• Word packs: Food, Cities, Proverbs, Holidays, Animals and School
• Build your collection of discovered words, with English and Amharic meanings

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
• Hints that teach: see the next logical step explained, not just the answer
• Short lessons and quizzes on fidel and Ge’ez numerals
• Classic mode with Ge’ez numerals (፩–፱)
• Hold a fidel to see how it sounds

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Notes, auto-notes, undo and duplicate highlighting
• Light, Dark and Parchment themes
• Statistics, streaks and achievements
• Optional daily reminder

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• Works fully offline, with no account needed
• No ads, no tracking: your progress stays on your device

Ge’ez Sudoku: a little logic, a little language.
```
